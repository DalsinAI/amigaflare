Flare: getting the game
=======================

This archive has the Flare engine for the Amiga and its default mod. The
game itself, Empyrean Campaign (its maps, art, music and sounds, about
610 MB), is not in it: get it from the Flare team, under its own licence
(CC BY-SA 3.0).

1. Download flare-game version 1.15, from
   https://github.com/flareteam/flare-game/releases/tag/v1.15
   (the "Source code" .zip or .tar.gz), on any computer.

2. From its mods drawer, copy the two drawers

       fantasycore
       empyrean_campaign

   into this drawer's mods drawer, next to "default". Keep mods/mods.txt
   as it is: it lists them in that order.

3. Double-click Flare.

Without the game, Flare starts with its default mod only, which has no
game to play.

Version 1.15 matters: a newer or older game may not suit this engine.
